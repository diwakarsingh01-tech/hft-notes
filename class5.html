4️⃣ Put Sell Kaise Karein — Step-by-Step Practical (6 Steps) [5:19–13:11]

Galti jo nahi karni: "Main hamesha put sell karne ki baat karta hoon. Naked positions bhi PE sell (put sell) hi."

Step 1: Stock gira hua check karo [5:50–6:14]

  • Account (Angel One / joh bhi) pe aao. Example: DAB (Dabur) ki put bechni hai.
  • Pehla step: kya Dabur gira hua hai? (Dono conditions check: upar se bhi gira + aaj bhi gira.)

Step 2: Strike selection — ATM put [6:25–6:46]

  • Conservative/normal play: normal play karte waqt ATM put sell karta hoon.
  • Future ≈ ₹409 [approx] ke aas-paas → round off karke 410 ki put = ATM.

Step 3: Instrument selection — NFO [6:49–7:50]

  • Dabur type karo → saare instruments: Dabur NSE stock, Dabur BSE stock.
  • Exchanges: NSE, BSE. Lekin stock options NFO mein hote hain: N = NSE, FO = Futures & Options category.
  • Futures — sirf 3 consecutive months: August mein → Aug, Sep, Oct; September mein → Sep, Oct, Nov. "Jis month mein baithhe ho us month se total 3 month ke hi future dikhenge, usse zyada nahi."
  • Index mein door ke bhi dikhte hain (6 mahine, 1 saal, 2 saal) — lekin wo liquid nahi hote.

Step 4: Market depth check — DAB 410 put [9:29–11:19]

  • India market mein European options hain, American nahi — Put = PE, Call = CE.
  • Market depth = 5 buyers + 5 sellers ki info.
  • Liquidity ka measure: buyer/seller price ka difference % — kam difference = zyada liquid.
Is case: ₹10 ke product mein 25 paise ka difference → 25 paise / ₹10 × 100 = 2.5% difference → LIQUID ✅ Agar difference 10%+ hota (buyer ₹8, seller ₹9) → buyer-seller "alag universe" → price discovery nahi → ILLIQUID ❌ 410 ki put liquid hai (<10%) → isme kaam kar sakte hain.

Step 5: Order placement — LIMIT ORDER ZAROORI [11:20–12:29]

⚠️ Market order stock options mein place NAHI kar sakte: Error: "Market orders are blocked for stock options due to illiquidity." Zerodha ye nahi keh raha ki ye wala option illiquid hai — saare stock options mein market order blocked hai. Index options mein market order allowed hai (liquidity better). → LIMIT ORDER lagao: market depth dekho (bid 9.45 / ask 9.70 [approx]) → "Main bol deta hoon — 9.65 pe agar mera order aaya to bech dena." After-market limit order: market band hai, par order broker ke paas chala gaya → broker subah khulte hi exchange se bech dega.

Step 6: EXIT / Profit Booking [12:34–13:11]

Example: DAB ki put ₹10.15 pe bechi thi → ab ₹9.50 ho gayi. Profit = 0.65 points × lot size ≈ ₹1600 [approx — 0.65 × lot; lot implied ~2461] → Exit pe click → LIMIT order ₹9.50 place karo → position exit. (Real-time market chalti waqt bhi exit kar sakte hain.)

5️⃣ Liquidity — Concept + Formula [8:02–9:28, 28:32–29:09]

  • Liquid ka matlab: "Jo cheez jitni zyada liquid hai, use kharidna aur bechna utna aasan hai."
  • Example — Stocks vs Real Estate: 2 crore ka plot chutki bajakar bech sakte ho? Nahi. 2 crore ke shares chutki bajate-bajate bik chuke honge.
  • Question easy/difficult ka nahi hai — sahi price milne ke chances ki hai. Stock market mein sahi price milne ke chances bahut zyada hote hain.
  • Illiquid: Buyer "₹1 ka kharidunga" | Seller "₹10 ka bechunga" → bechega hi nahi. "Options are illiquid instruments" (stock options comparatively).
📐 LIQUIDITY FORMULA (PPT): Spread % = (Ask − Bid) / Bid Agar > 5% → ILLIQUID Refined formula (teacher): Mid = (Bid + Ask) / 2 Spread % = (Ask − Bid) / Mid Agar > 5% → ILLIQUID, warna nahi (Note: example mein ~10% threshold bola tha (₹8/₹9), PPT 5% — dono note karo; 2.5% wala 410 put dono se liquid hai.)

6️⃣ DK (Decay) Rules — Profit Booking System [13:11–13:54]

7️⃣ Rollover — Loss Wali Position ka System [13:54–17:26]

📝 FULL WORKED EXAMPLE (Dabur 410): 1. ENTRY: ₹10 ki put sell ki (Dabur 410) 2. MONTH END: Dabur itna neeche gir gaya ki put ki keemat ₹15 ho gayi 3. BUY-BACK: 10 mein bechi, 15 mein buy karni padi → ₹5 LOSS 4. ROLL: Agle mahine ki SAME strike (410) ki put sell karo → 95% cases mein agle mahine ki put ₹15 se MEHENGI hi hogi (₹15.50/₹16/₹17) → kyunki pricing future ke basis pe calculate hoti hai → Chuninda cases mein (zyada ITM) kam ho sakti hai (₹14/₹13) → Lekin "₹15 ki buy karke ₹2 ki sell — AISA KABHI NAHI HONE WALA, KABHI NAHI" (2-4% ya 10% upar-neeche hi hoga) 5. Example: January put ₹15 pe buy (close) → February same put ₹16 pe sell
⚠️ ROLLED-OVER POSITIONS mein DK rules apply NAHI hote. "I repeat — rolled over positions mein hamare DK rules (30, 40, 70, 80 whatever) apply nahi hote."

Rollover position exit — do tarike [16:11–17:07]

✅ TARIKA 1 — Loss recovery + profit: ₹5 loss recover ho jaye (put ₹11 aaye) → uske upar jitna profit chahiye (₹5/₹10/₹20 shraddha) — yahan ₹5 aur → put ₹6 pe square off Math: 16 sell − 6 buy = ₹10 profit = ₹5 loss recovery + ₹5 profit ✅ TARIKA 2 — Pura theta chuso: "Main poora theta DK choosunga" — agar Dabur 410 se upar jaye month end tak → put 50 paise/peanuts pe → square off Math: 16 − 0.5 = ₹15.5 profit yahan se; ₹5 loss pichhle month → total ₹10.5 [approx] profit × lot size "Ek mahina loss hua, lekin overall agle mahine hi profit mein aa gaye."
💡 ROLLOVER ki definition (3-step process): put banayi → loss mein square off kiya → nayi put bechi = ROLL OVER. "Put selling rollover ka process sabko clear hai? Perfect." — "Isi tarike se put selling ki cycle chalti hai — repeatedly put bechte rehta hoon."

8️⃣ Strike Same + Put → Future Conversion [17:26–20:52]

  • Rollover mein strike SAME milegi — "Bilkul milegi! Strike price hamari same hi rahegi. Different kyun ho jayegi?"
  • Agar strike price illiquid hai TOH hum future mein convert karenge — ye transition point hai.
  • Scenario: stock gira hua tha → put sell ki → price aur gir gaya → overall position loss mein → main upar wali (strike) put bechta hoon → wo ITM put hoti hai → ⚠️ ITM put illiquid hoti hai.

Conversion ka TIMING — month end hi [20:35–20:59]

1️⃣7️⃣ Golden Rules (DOS / DON'TS)

✅ DOS

  1. 25L / 4 positions: ₹25L par max 4 naked positions, 4 different stocks, preferably 4 sectors — sirf leverage control ke liye, diversification nahi.
  2. Hamesha PE sell: Naked positions bhi put sell hi; put buying/gambling nahi.
  3. Stock gira hua ho: Us din ka loser + upar se gira hua + ATH se fall (large 20-25%, mid 30-35%, small 35-40%).
  4. Super 45 + Sales/Profit ATH: Non-negotiable; Net D/E < 0.2 (1Cr+ tier).
  5. ATM put normal play: Lower blue line ke around + ATM ke around hi sell karo.
  6. Limit order hi: Stock options mein market order blocked; hamesha limit order (spread + fill price bachata hai).
  7. Liquidity check: (Ask−Bid)/Mid > 5% → illiquid → kaam nahi; ITM put illiquid hoti hai.
  8. DK rules: 30% DK (1 din) / 50% (15 din) / 80% (month end se pehle) → square off.
  9. Rollover = 3-step: Sell → loss mein square off → same strike nayi put sell. Rollover positions mein DK rules apply NAHI hote.
  10. Rollover exit: Loss recover + jitna profit chahiye → book; ya poora theta chuso (expiry pe peanuts pe close).
  11. Future conversion: Sirf month-end (last 2-3-4 din) + bade loss pe; target = put sell price se 5% upar (buy price se nahi).
  12. Put rollover > future: Theta ke karan — future ka target kab aayega pata nahi.
  13. Leverage 2–2.5x: 25L → 4 positions (~24L exposure); 1Cr → 2Cr exposure; khali margin pe aur put mat becho.
  14. Book swings: Stock 5-6 din lagatar upar → book karo; big players 5-10% pe scalp karte hain.
  15. Goal = 20 saal: Market mein 20 saal tikna; stability > jaldi paisa.

❌ DON'TS

  1. 1 crore pe 1 crore ka exposure = stupidity (F&O leverage use hi nahi aaya).
  2. Khali margin dekh ke aur put bechna — "15% neeche wali, ATM ban jayegi... market 15% giregi aur 4 saal ka profit le jayegi" (maine logon ke saath dekha hai).
  3. ITM put kaam mat karo — illiquid hoti hai; 10-15%+ spread → future convert.
  4. Beech mahine jabardasti future mein convert nahi — sirf month-end.
  5. Candle-based put/call buying with SL-target = gambling — "4 baar banega, 5th mein sab jayega".
  6. WhatsApp teachers / "Bhagwan ke naam ke indicators" — Always use your brain.
  7. Small/mid cap (Inox Wind 5-6L loss, Crompton 4-4L loss) — <25L capital par Super 45 se bahar mat jao.
🐜 Golden quote: "Loss dekh ke dar jaaoge → rollover hi nahi kar paoge → rules follow nahi kar paoge → bade profit dikhenge kaise? Abhi aapko STABILITY pe focus karna hai."

1️⃣8️⃣ Quick Revision

Formulas / core math

Exposure = puts × notional per put (20 × ₹6L = ₹1.2Cr) Loss on fall = exposure × fall% (₹1.2Cr × 10% = ₹12L) Liquidity spread % = (Ask − Bid) / Mid → >5% = ILLIQUID DK% = (Sell price − Current price) ÷ Sell price × 100 Rollover: loss = buy-back − sell; agle month same strike sell (95% mehengi) Future target = PUT SELL price + 5% (base price, buy price se nahi) Market vs limit order cost = price diff × lot size (₹10 × 125 = ₹1250) Leverage sweet spot = 2x to 2.5x (25L → ~24L exposure; 1Cr → 2Cr)

✔ Super 45 + Sales/Profit ATH + us din ka LOSER → ATM put sell (limit order). ✔ Liquidity check: spread >5% = illiquid → nahi. ✔ Fresh put: 30% (1 din) / 50% (15 din) / 80% (month end) → square off. ✔ Loss mein → same-strike rollover (DK rules apply NAHI); exit = loss recover + profit, ya poora theta chuso. ✔ ITM illiquid → month-end future convert, target = put price + 5%. ✔ Leverage: 25L → 4 positions (2x).
