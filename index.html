2. Options Kya Hain? (Purpose + Wheat Story)

Stock market ka root purpose

  • Stock market long-term investors ke liye bana tha (business me paisa laga ke share profit).
  • 5 saal ke data me 90%+ stocks positive, 10 saal me ~99% positive. Isliye long-term investor = guaranteed money.
  • Market ka purpose intraday/swing nahi tha.

Options kyun aaye?

  • Stock price upar-neeche dance karta hai → log darte hain.
  • Ek party risk lena chahti hai (risk taker), doosri party risk se bachna chahti hai.
  • Options = risk transfer ek bande se doosre bande pe.
  • Options ka pehla use case commodities me aaya (gehun, chawal, dal, tel).

Wheat trader wali kahani (Put ka origin)

Gehun trader ke paas = 1,000 quintal gehun @ ₹4,000/quintal
Uska darr: price zyada gire to bada loss
Willing to lose: 200 × 1000 = ₹2,00,000  (3800 tak)
Limit: 3800 ke neeche jaana nahi chahiye

Solution: 3800 ki strike wali PUT option buy karo (insurance)
Put Option = Insurance. Jaise car/health insurance — agar market 3800 se neeche giri to gap ka paisa buyer ko milta hai. Nahi giri to premium kho jata hai (insurance lapse).

SURESH (Call Buyer) ke Scenarios

Scenario 1 — Price upar gayi, par sirf 4100

Price = 4100 → NOT above 4200 → call WORTHLESS
Loss = 20 × 1000 = ₹20,000

Scenario 2 — Price neeche giri (3900)

Suresh to sasta kharid raha hai — HAPPY (lekin call worthless)
Loss = 20 × 1000 = ₹20,000

Scenario 3 — Price ROCKET (4500)

Suresh ke paas jadooi taakat: chahe price 4500 ho, wo 4200 pe kharid sakta hai
Buy at 4200, Sell at 4500:
Gross  = 4500 − 4200 = ₹300/quintal
Minus premium = 300 − 20 = ₹280/quintal
NET PROFIT = 280 × 1000 = ₹2,80,000

RAMESH (Call Seller) — Teacher ki asli strategy

Background: Ramesh ke paas pehle se gehun @ ₹3,780 effective (put se premium mila)
Plan: 4200 pe bechna hai
Dalaal: "Waise bhi 4200 pe bechne wale ho — 4200 ki CALL sell karo @ ₹20"

Scenario 1 — Price 4200 nahi pahunchi

Call worthless → Ramesh ₹20 premium jeb me
Profit = 20 × 1000 = ₹20,000
("Rent kama raha hai — godown me gehun pada hai, rent mil raha hai")

Scenario 2 — Price 4500 pe chali gayi

Ramesh ko 4200 pe gehun bechna pada (waise bhi wahi karta)
+ ₹20 premium bhi jeb me
Effective SELL price = 4200 + 20 = ₹4,220

RAMESH KA FULL HISAAB:
Effective buy  = 3780
Effective sell = 4220
Net profit = (4220 − 3780) × 1000 = ₹4,40,000

Opportunity Loss concept ⚠️

Bina call ke → 4200 pe bechta → profit = 420 × 1000 = ₹4,20,000
Call ke saath → effective 4220 pe → profit = ₹4,40,000
Price 4500 bhi gaya → Ramesh ko sirf 4220 mila
Ye "₹280 × 1000 = ₹2,80,000" koi loss NAHI hai
→ Ye hai OPPORTUNITY LOSS (jo humein pata hi nahi tha wo hoga)
Ramesh apne hisse ka pani pee raha hai — happy
Call Seller ka KATRA (bina stock ke): 
Agar gehun nahi hai aur price 4500 gaya:
   Usko 4500 pe kharid ke 4200 pe dena padega

→ CALL tabhi sell karo jab stock/future ho (GOLDEN RULE)

6. Delivery, Margin & Rollover

Put SELLER ko delivery

Example: Dabur 450 PE, lot = 1250 shares
Agar expiry pe closing < 450 (10 paise bhi kam):
   → 1250 shares @ 450 ki delivery leni padegi
   → Account se debit: 450 × 1250 = ₹5,62,500
   → 1250 shares demat me automatically aayenge
Nahi le sakte → PENALTY + charges
Cash nahi hai → penalty

Call SELLER ko delivery (bina stock ke KATRA)

Agar stock call bechi aur stock nahi hai:
   → Broker auction market se stock khareedega
   → Auction market me stocks 10–20% MEHENGE
   → Call seller = bada loss

Margin (Sellers ke liye)

Option BUYER  = KOI margin nahi (sirf premium)
Option SELLER = MARGIN chahiye (~₹1.5 lakh per put)
Margin generate karke bhi milta hai:
   - Stocks pledge karke
   - FD / Liquid Bees pledge karke

Teacher ka rule (SABSE IMPORTANT)

"Main personally delivery kabhi nahi leta."
"Expiry se pehle hi position cut kar deta hoon — profit me to profit me, loss me to loss me." 
Rollover = purani position band + nayi same position next month
Loss book karke same strike ki nayi position = "loss ko delay karna"
2 saal tak rollover kar sakte ho
Same strike tab tak carry jab tak target na lage
Expiry se 1-2 din pehle hi rollover kar do

"TIME IS THE ENEMY OF OPTION BUYER, AND THE FRIEND OF OPTION SELLER."

Intraday me bhi theta

8. Liquidity, Spread & Order Placement

Liquidity kya hai

LIQUID   = buyer/seller ke beech gap kam → trade aasan
ILLIQUID = buyer/seller bahut door → trade mushkil

House example:
Seller ₹5 crore, Buyer ₹2 crore  → NO trade (illiquid)
Seller ₹5 crore, Buyer ₹4.99 cr  → trade likely (liquid)

Spread Formula

Mid price  = (Bid + Ask) / 2
Spread %   = (Ask − Bid) / Mid × 100

Reliance 1300 PE example

Reliance ≈ ₹1300 | 1300 PE:
Bid (buyer)  = ₹23.45
Ask (seller) = ₹24.00

Mid  = (23.45 + 24.00)/2 = ₹23.72
Diff = 24.00 − 23.45 = ₹0.55 (55 paise)
Spread % = 0.55 / 23.72 × 100 = 2.32% ≈ 2.3%
→ 2.3% < 5% → LIQUID ✅

Rules (Options vs Stocks)

InstrumentRule
OPTIONSspread >10% = ILLIQUID → avoid. Ideal <5%
STOCKS / FUTURESspread >1% = illiquid. (5 lakh ka 1% = ₹5,000 extra)

Market Depth example (11/13)

Bid = ₹11, Ask = ₹13
Mid = 12, diff = 2
Spread % = 2/12 = 16.7% → ILLIQUID ❌

Order kaise place karein

SELL karna hai → buyer (BID) se baat karo → Bid pe ya beech me price
BUY karna hai  → seller (ASK) se baat karo → Ask pe ya beech me price

KABHI MARKET ORDER nahi (options me liquidity kam)
HAMESHA LIMIT ORDER (analysis ke baad)

AMO (After Market Order):
Raat ko analysis karke limit order → agle din market khulte hi execute
Price upar gaya → order hit nahi (limit sirf tab tak)

Reliance 1250 PE (teacher ka real trade)

Analysis: Reliance 20% girta hai to rocket banta hai
Teacher: "1250 pe aaya to kharidne me interested"
Aaya nahi → 1250 PE SELL karo @ ~₹10
Spread: ~50 paise on ₹10 → 5% → LIQUID ✅

9. Settlement Prices (LTP vs Closing)

Trading ke dauran = LTP (Last Traded Price)
Expiry pe         = CLOSING PRICE = last 15 min ka volume-weighted price

Kyun closing price:
   → koi akela aakhri second me order daal ke price manipulate na kar sake
   → har second ka price × volume weight → average

Example (put seller ka reality)

Aapne put @ ₹20 bechi
Market 3-4 din upar gayi → put ka price ₹20 se girke ₹10
→ Aap chaho to turant buy-back karke ₹10 ka profit book kar sakte ho
→ END tak wait karne ki zaroorat nahi (30-50% early book karo)

Pura theta khana hai → expiry tak wait
Nahi to multiple profit booking possible (aage classes me)

Expiry pe option ki exact price

Put price at expiry  = max(0, Strike − Spot)
Call price at expiry = max(0, Spot − Strike)
Strike kisi bhi tick size ka multiple ho sakta hai (0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 1.00)

10. FUTURES — Deep Dive (All Examples + Math)

10.1 Futures Kya Hai (Basic)

FUTURES = ek contract:
"Aaj ka price lock karo, aur future me (1 mahine baad) usi price pe kharidoge/bechoge."

Forward vs Future:
Forward = private deal (do logon ke beech)
Future  = exchange pe standardised contract (NSE) — same lot, same expiry, same rules

10.2 Futures Kyun Bane?

(A) PRICE LOCK — payment baad me aayegi, par aaj hi price lock karna hai
(B) LEVERAGE — kam paise (margin) me bada exposure:
    ₹1 lakh margin → ₹4 lakh ka exposure
    Market +10% → profit ₹40,000 (na ki ₹10,000)
    Market −10% → loss ₹40,000
→ Leverage do-dhaari talwar: profit bhi double, loss bhi double

10.3 Example 1 — Leverage (Basic)

Capital (margin) = ₹1,00,000
Exposure         = ₹4,00,000  (4X leverage)

MARKET +10%:
Profit = 4,00,000 × 0.10 = ₹40,000  (40% return on margin)

MARKET −10%:
Loss   = 4,00,000 × 0.10 = ₹40,000  (40% loss)

10.4 Example 2 — Wheat Forward Contract + Arbitrage

Aaj 29 May 2026 | Gehun ₹4,000/quintal
Lena hai: 1000 quintal @ ₹4000
Margin (girvi) = ₹1 lakh  (baki paisa FD me)

INTEREST (risk-free arbitrage):
FD amount        = ₹4,00,000
Monthly interest = 4,00,000 × 0.5% = ₹2,000
→ 1 mahine me ₹2,000 risk-free jeb me = ARBITRAGE

MAHINE BAAD (expiry pe):
Paid = 4,000 × 1,000 = ₹40,00,000
Result = gehun ₹4,000/quintal pe MILA + ₹2,000 free interest

SCENARIOS:
Market @ 4000 → no profit/loss (sirf ₹2,000 interest)
Market @ 4500 → aap phir bhi ₹4,000 pe kharide = BAHUT profit
Market @ 3600 → aap phir bhi ₹4,000 pe kharide = ₹4,00,000 loss

MAGAR: free lunch nahi milta!
Market future ko ₹4,000 pe nahi, ₹4,020 pe bechega (spot + 0.5%)
→ Arbitrage pehle hi price me shamil hota hai

10.5 Example 3 — Future Price Formula + Dabur

FORMULA:
FUTURES PRICE = SPOT + INTEREST COST − DIVIDEND COST

DABUR REAL EXAMPLE:
Spot = ₹443 | June Future = ₹451 (1 mahina)

Expected future = 443 + (443 × 0.5%) = 443 + 2.22 = ₹445.22
Gap actual = 451 − 443 = ₹8
Extra gap = 8 − 2.2 ≈ ₹5.8 → Bid-Ask spread ki wajah se

NORMAL RULE: future me spot se ~0.5% (₹2-3) ka hi gap hota hai

WIPRO DIVIDEND CASE:
Agar dividend ₹5 aane wala hai:
Futures = Spot + Interest − Dividend
        = 100 + 1 − 5 = ₹96  (future SPOT se SASTA)
→ "Sasta future" = dividend adjust hua hai, free paisa NAHI

10.6 Example 4 — Dabur Lot + Margin (5X Leverage)

Dabur future lot  = 1,250 shares
Price             = ₹450 (approx)
Contract value    = 1,250 × 450 = ₹5,62,500 (≈₹5.6 lakh)
Margin            = ₹1,00,000

Leverage = 5,62,500 ÷ 1,00,000 = 5.6X  (teacher: "5X")

MARKET +10%:
Profit = 5,62,500 × 0.10 = ₹56,250  = 56% return on margin

MARKET −10%:
Loss   = 5,62,500 × 0.10 = ₹56,250  = 56% loss

+ Taxes: buy ₹47 / sell ₹300 → ~₹350-500/month

10.7 Example 5 — Mark-to-Market (MTM) Daily

Account me cash = ₹10,000
Buy future @ ₹4 lakh exposure

DAY 1 — Market +1%:
Profit = 4,00,000 × 1% = ₹4,000 → broker CREDIT kar dega
Cash   = 10,000 + 4,000 = ₹14,000

DAY 2 — Market −5%:
Loss = 4,00,000 × 5% = ₹20,000 → broker DEBIT karega
Cash = 14,000 − 20,000 = −₹6,000 ← CASH NEGATIVE!

CASH NEGATIVE PE:
1. Broker interest charge karta hai
2. Brokerage double
3. Zyada negative → pledged stocks bech deta hai

RULE: Margin ke saath hamesha CASH BUFFER rakho (₹50k-1L extra)

10.8 Example 6 — Bid-Ask Spread (Reliance)

Reliance 1300 PE:
Bid = ₹23.45 | Ask = ₹24.00
Mid = (23.45+24)/2 = ₹23.72
Diff = 0.55 | Spread % = 0.55/23.72 = 2.32% → LIQUID ✅

ILLIQUID (11/13): spread = 2/12 = 16.7% → AVOID ❌

10.9 Example 7 — Stock Call Sell (Physical Delivery Khatra)

Call SELL kiya (4200) bina stock ke, price 4500:
Loss = (4500 − 4200) × lot = 300 × 1250 = ₹3,75,000

Auction market (10-20% mehenga):
Auction price ≈ 4500 + 15% = ₹5,175

Index me cash settled → delivery ka jhanjhat nahi

10.10 Example 8 — Delivery Bachao Trick (Teacher)

Problem: Stock put bechi → stock gira → delivery leni padegi
Delivery cash needed = 3800 × 1250 = ₹47,50,000 (47 lakh!)

Solution: SAME stock ka FUTURE BUY karo
→ Put ka loss/settlement future se adjust ho jayega
→ Delivery ka jhanjhat khatam
→ Sirf margin (~₹1 lakh) lagta hai, poora ₹47 lakh nahi

"Delivery kabhi nahi leta — expiry se 1-2 din pehle rollover/square-off"

10.11 Example 9 — Rollover Math (2-3 Mahine)

Aapne June future buy kiya @ ₹451, plan 3 mahine ka
Expiry se 1-2 din pehle:
   Step 1: June future SELL @ ₹445 (book current P&L)
   Step 2: July future BUY @ ₹447 (naya contract)
   → Position bani rahi, bas mahina badla

Cost of roll: buy ₹47 + sell ₹300 ≈ ₹350/roll
2-3 mahine = ₹700-1,000 taxes
→ "Door ka future buy karo" taaki roll kam karna pade

11. Futures Use Cases (Real Applications)

Use Case 1 — Hedging (Stocks ko protect karna)

Scenario: Reliance ke 1000 shares hain, darr hai 2 mahine giregi, bechna nahi
Solution: Reliance ka 2-mahine ka future SELL karo
Market giri → shares loss, future profit → BALANCE
Market upar → shares profit, future loss → BALANCE
Fayda: stocks hold, dividend milta hai, price risk protected

Use Case 2 — Leverage ke saath Directional bet

Lagna hai Dabur 3 mahine me 15% upar
1250 shares ka kharcha = ₹5.6 lakh | Aapke paas ₹1 lakh
Dabur future BUY @ ₹1 lakh margin → exposure ₹5.6 lakh
15% upar → profit = ₹84,000 (84% return!)
⚠️ 10% neeche → ₹56,000 loss + margin call ka risk

Use Case 3 — Delivery se bachne ke liye (teacher ka trick)

Put bechi, stock gira → delivery instead of cash
Same stock ka future buy karo:
   Put ka loss/settlement future se adjust
   Cash lagta hai sirf margin (~1 lakh), na ki poora amount

Use Case 4 — Arbitrage (kyun nahi milta)

Theoretically: Spot ₹443, 1-mahine future ₹445.20
Agar future ₹446 pe bech raha hai → buy spot + sell future = ₹2 profit?
NAHI! Arbitrageurs pehle hi gap kha jate hain
Sasta/mehenga future = koi reason hai (dividend/interest) — FREE LUNCH NAHI

Use Case 5 — Rollover (position aage badhana)

June future buy kiya, thesis 3 mahine ka
Expiry se pehle: June sell + July buy = ROLLOVER
Har roll pe tax (~₹350) lagta hai
Future me roll 2-3 mahine tak hi (exchange sirf 3 serial months deta hai)

12. Futures MTM & Cash Negative (Detail)

FUTURES = har roz hisaab (MTM), expiry pe nahi

Step-by-step:
Din 1: Future buy @ ₹450 | Market +1% → price 454.5
       Profit = 1250 × (454.5 − 450) = ₹5,625 → CREDIT account
Din 2: Market −5% → price 431.8
       Loss = 1250 × (454.5 − 431.8) = ₹28,375 → DEBIT
       Cash nahi → CASH NEGATIVE

Cash negative pe:
1. Interest charge
2. Brokerage double
3. Pledged stocks bech diye jate hain

Isliye:
- Hamesha cash buffer rakho (₹50k-1L extra)
- Comfortable capital: ₹12 lakh+ (4-5 lakh = sirf 1 lot)
Leverage ki cost hamesha hoti hai:
  • Futures me interest cost hidden hai (future price me shamil)
  • MTF (Margin Trading Facility) me interest VISIBLE charge hota hai
  • Market leverage FREE me nahi deti

13. Formulas + Golden Rules + Quick Revision

All Formulas

OPTIONS:
PUT BUYER payout      = max(0, Strike − Spot) − premium   [loss limited to premium]
PUT SELLER            = +premium; loss = max(0, Strike − Spot) − premium
CALL BUYER payout     = max(0, Spot − Strike) − premium   [loss limited to premium]
CALL SELLER           = +premium; loss = max(0, Spot − Strike) − premium
Effective buy (put seller)   = Strike − premium
Effective sell (call seller) = Strike + premium
Option price at expiry       = max(0, |Spot − Strike|)
Spread %                     = (Ask − Bid) / Mid × 100

FUTURES:
FUTURES PRICE = SPOT + INTEREST (0.5%/month) − DIVIDEND
LEVERAGE      = CONTRACT VALUE ÷ MARGIN  (4-6X typical)
PROFIT/LOSS   = EXPOSURE × %MOVE
MTM           = daily credit/debit of P&L

Golden Rules (Options)

  1. Hamesha SELLER bano — "Always, always, always be an option seller."
  2. Bina stock/future ke CALL kabhi sell mat karo (auction 10-20% mehenga).
  3. Delivery kabhi mat lo — expiry se pehle rollover/square-off.
  4. Loss book karke same strike roll karo ("loss ko delay karna").
  5. Illiquid option me kaam mat karo (spread >10%).
  6. Market order kabhi nahi, hamesha limit order.
  7. Hedge karo — OTM put buy = portfolio insurance.
  8. Capital: ₹12 lakh+ comfortable option selling (4-5 lakh = sirf 1 lot).
  9. FrontPage app pe paper trading karo (bina paise ke practice).

Golden Rules (Futures)

  1. Leverage = do-dhaari talwar — loss bhi double.
  2. Cash buffer hamesha rakho (MTM ke liye).
  3. Sasta future = koi reason hai (dividend) — free lunch nahi.
  4. Tax cost ₹47 buy / ₹300 sell — door ka buy karo.
  5. Stock call sell bina stock/future ke kabhi nahi.
  6. Capital: ₹12 lakh+ comfortable, 4-5 lakh = sirf 1 lot.
  7. Expiry se pehle square-off ya roll — delivery/penalty se bacho.

Ek-line Definitions

OPTIONS TRADING = Transfer of RISK from buyer to seller
PUT OPTION      = Right to SELL at predefined price (buyer) / obligation to BUY (seller)
CALL OPTION     = Right to BUY at predefined price (buyer) / obligation to SELL (seller)
"Right, not obligation" — buyer can walk away
EXPIRY          = Last Tuesday, 3:30 PM; contract value becomes ZERO
PREMIUM         = Cost of option (insurance price)
STRIKE          = Predefined exercise price
THETA           = Time decay — buyer ka enemy, seller ka friend
MARGIN          = Sellers pay (~₹1.5L per put), buyers don't
SPREAD          = (Ask − Bid)/Mid × 100; <5% options = liquid
