5️⃣ Profit Booking Rules (Fresh vs Rolled) ⭐ [11:08–16:40]

Notation: F = Fresh put sell (nayi put), R = Rolled-over put sell. DK = premium decay (premium ka girna).

Fresh Put (F) — 3-tier exit ladder

ConditionAction
1 din mein 30% decay (DK)Turant buy back + square off
1 din mein 30% nahi15 din mein 50% DK par square off
70–80% DKKabhi bhi exit karo, koi issue nahi
Example (fresh put): ₹100 par bechi → 1 din mein 30% DK → ₹70 par buy back → Profit = 30 × lot size (Nifty lot = 65) = ₹1,950/lot ✅ "30 × lot size" — teacher: "Absolutely correct."

Rolled-over Put (R) — kab square off

  • Example: Pehli put 24,000 strike par bechi → market strike ke neeche gaya → roll over karte raho (same strike 24,000, next month). Base price = 24,000 (jis strike se selling start ki).
  • Jab Nifty 24,000 (base) se upar jaye expiry ke aas-paas → tum shaandaar profit mein → saare trades khatam karo.
  • 30/40/50% DK rule rolled-over put par LAGU NAHI hota — rolled put tab tak rakho jab tak wo upar na jaye.
  • Alternative (track-record method): Track rakho — "1 mahine ₹10,000 profit, agle mahine ₹20,000 loss, ab ₹70,000 profit" → tab cut kar sakte ho. Saare losses recover ho chuke hon + tagde profit mein ho → tab hi rolled position off karke naya trade lo.

Expiry rule

📌 ONLY MONTHLY expiry mein kaam. Weekly KABHI NAHI. Exception: agar monthly mein liquidity na mile — tab hi weekly.

"Chul" ho to (gun-on-head scenario)

  • Chul honi hi nahi chahiye. Agar hai → neeche wali (OTM) put becho (support level ke aas-paas, ~23,100 ya ~23,600 [approx]).
  • Terminology: Jahan Nifty abhi hai wahi ki put = ATM. Nifty upar hai → OTM put becho. Nifty gir chuka hai → ATM ya kai baar ITM put bhi bech deta hai.
💡 DK clarification (Devendra ji): "70-80% DK par any-time sell → sirf 30% profit mila?" → "Yaar tum ULTA samajh rahe ho." ₹100 par becha → ₹70 par buy back → ₹30 profit 1 din mein = 30% DK = 30% profit. 70-80% DK = premium ₹20-30 reh gaya → 70-80% profit (seller ke liye DK% = profit%). "18 din hold karke last 5 din ka juice (cream) mat phenko" — last 3-4 din mein DK bahut tezi se aata hai (index ek direction set ho jaati hai) — isliye last dinon mein 70-80% DK chahiye, sirf 30% nahi.

7️⃣ Settlement — Options vs Futures (Daily MTM) ⭐ [59:42–1:03:09]

OPTIONS (settled at expiry)

  • Agar option trade mein ₹1 lakh ka loss dikh raha ho → sirf margin requirement badhegi.
  • Account se ₹1 lakh nahi katega (expiry tak unrealized rehta hai). Sab kuch expiry par settle hota hai.

FUTURES (daily settlement / mark-to-market)

Day 1: Future neeche gaya → ₹10,000 loss → USI DIN account se ₹10,000 cut (cash component se) Day 2: Future upar aaya → ₹3,000 profit → trading ledger mein wapas add Har din aise ghat-badh hota rehta hai = Mark to Market (MTM)

SettlementExpiry parDaily (MTM)

🚨 GOLDEN RULE — Cash kabhi negative na karo: MTM cash component se jaata hai. Agar cash component negative ho jaye → broker 18–20% annual interest charge karta hai + brokerage complications. "We should make sure cash kabhi negative na jaye." (18% of ₹1L = ₹18,000/year ~ ₹1,500/month — dhyaan rakho, galat math se bacho.)

8️⃣ European vs American System [44:36–46:19]

  • American system: Option ko kabhi bhi (anytime) exercise kar sakte ho.
  • European system: Sirf expiry par exercise/square-off hota hai.
🚗 Car insurance analogy: American insurance: "Gaadi jab bhi thuke, tab bhi claim kar sakte ho" → anytime claim. European insurance: "Main sirf 31 tarikh ko claim karne dunga (expiry 31 hai)" → sirf expiry par. India: Physical/cash settlement — sab expiry par hota hai (automatic square-off expiry par).
⚠️ Important nuance: Expiry se pehle manual square-off kabhi bhi kar sakte ho — market mein buyers/sellers available hain, contract unhe transfer kar sakte ho. Sirf automatic/exercise part expiry par bound hai. (Student: "matlab automatic square-off expiry par hi hoga" → Teacher: "Absolutely correct. Bilkul.")

9️⃣ Margin, Pledging & Cash Collateral (50% Rule) [1:05:11–1:06:37]

Margin call ka practical kaam (Vikas ji)

  • Margin call unhe aati hai jinke paas ₹10 lakh ho aur wo poora amount deploy kar dein.
  • ₹25 lakh par sirf 4 positions → margin call kabhi nahi aayegi.
  • Margin = base margin (position maintain karne ke liye) + kuch loss buffer. Loss bada ho gaya → exchange bolega "aur margin rakho, warna position kaat denge." (Zerodha notification: "margin reached X% of your position, hum square off kar denge.")

Two Components of Margin (Lalit ji)

  1. Cash component: Liquid cash / Liquid beech / Liquid Case / debt funds — inhe pledge karo.
  2. Non-cash component: Shares pledge karo (large-cap jaise Bajaj Finance).
🚨 EXCHANGE RULE: Minimum 50% cash ya cash component chahiye; baaki 50% equity collateral se la sakte ho. Ye har margin-wali cheez par lagta hai (future buy, call sell, kuch bhi). "Maximum 50, minimum 50."
  • Option BUYING: Pura cash se buy hota hai — teacher ke students buy nahi karte, sirf square-off ke liye buy karte hain.
  • Liquid Case ek ETF hai (LIQUIDCASE) — Grow se, Zerodha se, kahin se bhi kharid sakte ho. Teacher: "Maine Grow mein kiya hua hai — 30 lakh ka."
  • Mutual funds se bhi margin milta hai (purane MF bhi count hote hain). Direct MF ko Zerodha mein Coin se transfer (2-3 din) ya bech ke buy karo (tax lagega).
  • Pledging practical aage dikhayenge (session mein).

🔟 Rollover Charges & Far-Month Futures [1:03:13–1:05:11]

  • Rollover is EXPENSIVE: Future buy + sell karne par sarkar/broker bahut saare charges leti hai: STT, SEBI tax, Brokerage, Cess, GST, PTT.
  • Poora rollover cycle (ye future becha → next future khareeda) ko ~₹3,000 costly banati hai [approx].
  • Teacher ka rule: Immediate month ki jagah door se door ka liquid future lo (e.g. August chhod ke seedha September) → baar-baar trade nahi lena padta → charges kam.

Rollover = 2 kaam (koi "rollover button" nahi hota): 1. Purani position OFF karo (buy back) 2. Nayi position CREATE karo (next month same strike sell) Ek hi tick mein nahi hota — pehle off, phir create.

Covered call concept

  • Bahut saari puts bechi → bahut saare futures accumulate ho gaye. Future upar-neeche dance karta hai → upar ki strike ki call sell karo.
  • Example: Dabur 400 par liya → 500 ki call sell. Price neeche raha → call mein profit; upar gaya → call mein loss — koi dikkat nahi, kyunki future mein bada profit → overall profit.
  • BUT condition: Call tabhi becho jab stock holding LOT SIZE ke multiple mein ho. ₹6 lakh ka stock hai to 1 call bech sakte ho — koi dikkat nahi. (Warning: ₹1 lakh stock + call bechi → call ITM hui to negative ₹6 lakh exposure.)
  • Teacher ka style: "Main call rarely bechta hoon. I am in favor of selling PUT only. Main put bechta rehta hoon, phir future buy karke future ko rollover karta rehta hoon — kyunki call mein mufli milti hai." (Market neeche aa chuka hota hai jab future buy karte ho → call already lower value par → call bechne par kuch nahi milega.)
🚫 Blunder warning (Pooja ji): "Dabur future 442 par sell kiya + HDFC Bank future sell" → Teacher: "Main confident nahi. Main put sell karta hoon, future nahi. Ye blunder mat karna." — Put/future/call ek dusre nahi hain.
💡 Confidence rule: Confident nahi → INDEX mein kaam karo (guaranteed upar aata hai, zyada girta nahi, stable returns, kam volatility). Confident ho tabhi stocks mein. Dabur proof: Pichhle 1 saal (2025 se ab tak) Dabur mein sirf 2 loss-making trades — isliye har class mein "Dabur" muh se nikalta hai.

1️⃣4️⃣ Golden Rules (DOS / DON'TS)

✅ DOS

  1. Bada paisa = simple cheezein, consistently — rocket science nahi.
  2. Index lagatar upar ja raha ho → kuch mat karo; recent high se 2-4-5% girne do, phir put becho.
  3. Put tabhi becho jab stock/index recently gira ho — premium zyada milega.
  4. Orange line (Envelope) ke neeche = ALWAYS kaam; upar = kaam hi nahi.
  5. Only monthly expiry. Weekly = sirf jab monthly mein liquidity na mile.
  6. Fresh put: 1-din 30% DK → exit; 15-din 50% → exit; 70-80% → kabhi bhi exit.
  7. Rolled put: % rules nahi; base strike ke upar jaane par saare trades band karo (ya jab poori track-record solid profit mein ho).
  8. Adjustments kabhi nahi — "shaitan ka kaam hai." Trade profit mein hi kato.
  9. Rollover = 2 steps (old off + new create); koi button nahi.
  10. Last 3-4 din ka juice mat phenko — decay tezi se aata hai.
  11. Covered call sirf tab jab holding lot-size multiple mein ho (₹6L stock → 1 call).
  12. Balance in strikes: 25L → 4 positions (2 ATM + 1 ITM + 1 OTM style); 5-6L → 1 position.
  13. Confident nahi → index; confident → stocks. Dabur jaise stable stock par hi risk.
  14. Chart hamesha equity ka dekho (future buy karte waqt bhi).
  15. Cash collateral: liquid beech/Liquid Case, debt funds, shares, MF — sab pledge karo; min 50% cash/cash component har margin-wali position ke liye.
  16. Rules kabhi bend mat karo — jahan bend kiya, wahan loss hua (teacher ka personal experience).

❌ DON'TS

  1. All-time high par buy + SL na lagana → wipe out (market life 3-4 saal).
  2. Bottom par buy karke SL lagana — sabse badi bewakoofi.
  3. Cash component negative mat hone do — 18-20% annual interest + brokerage complications.
  4. Weekly expiry mein kaam mat karo — hamesha month-end wali put.
  5. Option buying mat karo — sirf square-off ke liye buy karo.
  6. Future sell ko put sell samajhna = blunder. (Pooja ji warning: Dabur future sell.)
  7. Adjustments kabhi nahi.
  8. ₹1 premium wali OTM bechke bada risk mat lo — chinti jaisa khao, haathi jaisa mat do.
  9. Margin ka kam se kam 3% return nahi wala trade mat lo (aage definition aayegi).
🐜🐘 Golden quote: "Option seller eats like ants, shits like elephant." — Chinti ki tarah khate hain, haathi ki tarah nikaalte hain. Mat karo ye: 410/400 strikes par ₹100 premium (chinti jaisa) leke risk lena aur phir ek sath ₹1 lakh dena (haathi jaisa). Isliye teacher ek sath ₹20-30k kamane ki koshish karta hai (index mein).
