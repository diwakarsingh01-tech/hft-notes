1. Class 1 Recap (Revision)

RECAPJo sab Class 1 mein cover hua tha — quick revision:

  • Why market exists? Options/derivatives ka real purpose = risk transfer. Option buyer ke sar se risk nikal kar option seller ke sar pe chala jata hai.
  • Put Option = Right to Sell (insurance). Log put isliye buy karte hain ki agar stock gir gaya to unke losses avoid ho jaayein. Put seller isliye bechta hai ki agar stock gira to delivery lena hai, nahi gira to premium kha lunga.
  • Call Option = Right to Buy at predefined price. Jo log call sell karte hain (jaise agar mere paas stocks/futures hain) — agar stock strike se upar gaya to predefined price pe maal bech dunga; nahi gaya to koi dikkat nahi.
  • Futures = Leverage tool. ~₹1 lakh margin girvi rakh ke ~₹4–5 lakh exposure (4–5x typical leverage).
  • Liquidity — hamesha liquid contracts mein hi kaam karo, illiquid mein nahi.
  • Options trading = transfer of risk from buyer to seller.
  • New words: MTM, Rollover, Strike Price, Premium, Theta, Option Buyer, Option Seller, Put-Call-Strike.
Day 1 Wrap-up: "We are eating theta — hum time value of money kha rahe hain. Options exist to transfer risk. Time ke basis pe option ka price apne aap girta hai jo seller ki jeb mein jata hai." Theta ki detail Greeks mein aaj karenge.

2.1 Put Option ke Strikes (DAB @ ₹450)

StrikeTypeKyun?
₹470 PutITM (In the Money)Strike (470) > current price (450). Agar abhi exercise karu to ₹470 pe sell ho sakta hai jabki market ₹450 hai → intrinsic value hai.

Logical check (why 470 put can't be ₹2): Market ₹450, 470 put ₹2 ho toh — buy put ₹2, buy stock ₹450, exercise karke sell ₹470 → ₹18 risk-free profit! No Free Lunch Theorem → aisi cheez market mein exist nahi kar sakti. Isliye 470 put ki minimum value = ₹20 (470 − 450).

2.2 Call Option ke Strikes (DAB @ ₹450)

StrikeTypeKyun?
₹430 CallITMStrike (430) < current price (450). Buy karne ka right ₹430 pe jabki market ₹450 → intrinsic value ₹20.
₹450 CallATMStrike = current price.
₹470 CallOTMStrike (470) > current price (450).
Easy memory: Put ka case = upar wali ITM. Call ka case = ulta, neeche wali ITM.

2.3 Moneyness ki Terms

  • Moneyness = Intrinsic Value. If moneyness 0 → OTM/ATM; if positive → ITM.
  • ITM contract = usme intrinsic value hai. Isliye "In the Money".
  • Spot-strike gap pe options 10-10 ke multiples mein hote hain (ya 5-5), isliye ATM ko round-off karke dekhte hain.
  • Indian markets mein ITM contracts usually illiquid hote hain. Big players ki strategies ATM + OTM pe chalti hain (market-making, next-level strategies).

3. Intrinsic Value vs Time Value

Har option price mein do components hote hain: Intrinsic Value (IV) + Time Value (TV).

Formulas:
Put → IV = max(Strike Price − Current Price, 0)
Call → IV = max(Current Price − Strike Price, 0)

IV kabhi negative nahi ho sakti — ya toh zero, ya positive. Isliye "max(x, 0)".

DAB ka June future ₹451 pe close hua. Options ki saari calculation future price ke respect mein hoti hai (stock nahi), kyunki future ~0.5% premium hota hai. Toh current market price = ₹451.

4. Payoff — 4 Legs (Long/Short Call & Put)

Every position is built on 4 legs. Har strategy in char payoff ke combination se banti hai.

LegMatlabRiskProfitKis direction pe profit
Long Call (Buy Call)Premium pay karke call buyLimited (premium)Unlimited (upar)Market upar (big up move)
Short Call (Sell Call)Call sell (covered ya naked)Unlimited (stock upar ja sakta hai)Limited (premium)Flat / down
Long Put (Buy Put)Insurance — premium payLimited (premium)Unlimited (neeche)Market girne pe
Short Put (Sell Put)Premium collectLarge (par unlimited nahi — stock 0 se upar hi rukega)Limited (premium)Flat / up
Short Put vs Short Call risk difference: Stock ₹0 bhi ho jaye, put seller ka max loss = strike × lot size (limited number, e.g. ₹5 lakh). Par stock upar 10,000x bhi jaa sakta hai (hypothetically) → call seller ka loss crores tak. Isliye call seller = unlimited risk, put seller = large par limited risk.

4.1 Long Call Example (Math)

Buy 420 Call @ ₹10, Lot size 100.
Max Loss = ₹10 × 100 = ₹1,000 (premium gone)
Max Profit = Unlimited (market upar badhta jaye)
Break-even = Strike + Premium = 420 + 10 = ₹430

Market 430 tak aayi → no profit no loss.
Market 430 se upar → profit.
Market 420 se neeche (ya 430 tak) → loss, max ₹1,000.

Kab use karein: jab trader ko lagta hai bada rally aane wala hai. (Note: Hemant Jain call buy nahi karta.)

4.2 Short Put Example (Seller's Bread & Butter)

"Ye meri bread & butter hai — pichhle 5 saal se yahi karta hoon." — Short put: premium collect. Market upar, neeche (halka), ya sideways — sab mein profit. Sirf tab loss jab market strike se kaafi neeche gir jaye.

4.3 Short Call Example

Stock ₹380 pe buy kiya. 440 Call sell kiya — "agar market ₹440 tak gaya, delivery dene mein khush hoon". Agar market 480 gayi aur share nahi hai paas, toh ₹480 mein buy karke ₹440 pe delivery deni padegi → loss.

Why 99% of retailers are option buyers: hero-zero khelte hain — ₹2 ka option buy karo, ₹100 ho jaye toh maza. (But buyer ka win-probability kam hota hai; seller theta se win karta hai.)

5. Greeks — Delta, Theta, Gamma, Vega, Rho

Option premium ek nahi, paanch alag-alag taqaton se hilta hai. Inhi ko "Greeks" kehte hain.

GreekFull Form / MatlabRule of Thumb
DeltaSensitivity to change in stock price (₹1 stock move → option kitna move)ATM = 0.5 (always), ITM = 0.8–0.9, OTM = 0.1–0.3
ThetaChange in option price because 1 day passed (time value of money)ATM max (e.g. 0.30), ITM/OTM kam (~0.10)
GammaChange in delta because of change in stock priceHamesha positive (long); short gamma = danger
VegaChange in option price because of change in IV (implied volatility)IV high → premium fat (zyada)
RhoChange in option price because of change in risk-free interest rateIndia ke short-dated options ke liye minor

5.1 Delta — Detail

  • Definition: ₹1 stock price move → option price kitna badlega. (Sensitivity to change in stock price.)
  • ATM delta always = 0.5. DAB ₹450 pe → 450 put ka delta ≈ 0.5.
  • DAB ₹1 gira → put ka price 50 paise badhega (put buyer fayda market girne pe → put delta negative, opposite direction).
  • Call delta positive (market upar → call price upar), Put delta negative.
  • ITM delta 0.8–0.9 (stock ₹1 move → option ₹0.80–0.90), OTM delta 0.1–0.3 (₹20 door OTM barely moves, ~10 paise).
  • Delta ≈ probability of expiring ITM (teacher ne short note kiya, detail nahi).
  • ITM 470 put ke paas ₹20 intrinsic hi hai; DAB ₹1 girne pe intrinsic ₹21 → option ≈ ₹1 move hota hai.
ATM 450 Put (delta −0.5): DAB ₹4 gira → put ₹2 mehengi. DAB ₹4 badha → put ₹2 sasti.
OTM 430 Put (delta ~0.1–0.2): DAB gira → sirf 5–10 paise move.
Seller ka Delta: Hemant ki sab strategies mein delta positive hota hai. Wo put sell karta hai → put ka delta negative tha, wo usse bech deta hai → uske liye delta positive (market upar → fayda).

5.2 Theta — Detail

  • Definition: Change in option price because one day passed (baaki sab constant). "Time value of money" = isi ka naam.
  • Ek din beetne ka koi option price girta hi girta hai (agar sab constant rahe).
  • Example numbers: ATM theta 0.30, ITM 0.10, OTM 0.10.
ATM ₹5 ka option → agla din ₹4.70 (theta 0.30 gone)
OTM ₹2 ka option → agla din ₹1.90
ITM ₹20 ka option → agla din ₹19.90
Theta negative for buyers, positive for sellers. Buyer pays theta, seller collects. "That's why sellers usually always make money."

5.3 Gamma — Detail

  • Definition: Change in delta because of change in stock price. (Delta change hone se option price kitna badla.)
  • Market jis direction mein jaati hai, usi direction mein delta accumulate hota jaata hai (relative terms mein).
  • Put gamma positive: DAB 450 put delta −0.5; DAB girne par contract ITM ho jata hai → delta −0.9 (magnitude badha). Same direction move.
  • Call gamma positive: 450 call delta 0.5; DAB ₹10 badha → call ITM → delta 0.76+ (badha).
  • Gamma both positive (long). Buy option = long gamma. Sell option = short gamma.

Gamma Blast / Short Gamma (Teacher ka straight talk)

"Gamma Blast ek faltu concept hai — waste of time. Pankaj Sahu type scams mein ye words use hote hain." Jo hota hai wo "delta blast" hai: short-gamma wale (jo call+put dono bech dete hain, delta ≈ 0) pe market kisi bhi direction mein move kare, delta ek taraf pile-up ho jata hai aur unke bade loss hote hain. Yeh market ka normal gamma behavior hai, koi magic nahi.

5.4 Vega & Rho — Short

  • Vega: IV/volatility change se option price badalna. Volatility badhe → sab premium badhenge (insurance analogy: gusse wala aadmi zyada premium).
  • Rho: Interest rate change (e.g. RBI rate badhaya) → option price pe minor positive impact. India mein short-dated options ke liye negligible. (US mein long-dated LEAPS/swaps exist karte hain.)

6. Implied Volatility & India VIX

6.1 India VIX

  • India VIX = volatility/fear index. Number jitna zyada, utni zyada volatility/fear; jitna kam, utni stability.
  • VIX calculate hota hai — kai companies ke ATM call+put premiums mein jump se, weighted average + complex calculations (reverse Black-Scholes).

6.2 BIG Misconception (Important!)

StatementAsli Sach
"Options price increases because VIX increased"GALAT (but books/NISM exam mein isse true mark karo!)
"VIX increases because option prices increased"SAHI
Logic: VIX ko kisne badhaya? Option premium pe pehle change aata hai — news/Trump-type events se logon mein fear badhta hai → traders premium badha dete hain (demand-supply) → phir VIX calculate hoke upar jaata hai. Pehle premium, phir VIX. 99% books galat likhti hain. Exam mein jo standard statement hai wahi mark karna.

6.3 Black-Scholes Model (Knowledge only)

C = S·N(d₁) − K·e^(−rt)·N(d₂)
Where: C = call price, S = stock price, N = normal distribution area, K = strike, r = risk-free rate, t = time to expiry, d₁/d₂ = complex log terms (ln(S/K) + ...), σ (sigma) = volatility.
  • Teacher ne IIT Delhi Financial Mathematics course mein ye derivation ki thi (10 pages!). Deep mein jaana zaroori nahi.
  • IV kaise nikalte hain: Market call price de deta hai → baaki sab values (time, S, K, r) known hain → software mein reverse-Black-Scholes karke sigma (IV) nikalte hain. Har liquid stock ke liye, weighted average → India VIX.
  • "Stock market itna complex math use karta hai — retail ko lagta hai paisa asaan hai. Asaan nahi hai."
Teacher ka fair price: "Main jo price use karta hoon = (Bid + Ask)/2. Jo market kehti hai, sahi kehti hai. Uske according strategy likhta hoon."

8. Open Interest (OI), Max Pain, PCR

8.1 Open Interest (OI)

  • OI = kitne open contracts exist karte hain. Contracts create (buyer+seller dono) aur delete hote rehte hain → OI total open count.
  • Who creates contracts? Buyer aur seller dono simultaneously. (Buyer nahi → seller kise bechega.)
  • Rain/₹5-₹10 bet example (lottery-like contract system): 2 contracts created = OI 2, +10 = OI 12, etc.
  • Bohot zyada OI → market manipulation risk → SEBI/exchange regulations.
  • OI reading (DAB): 430 pe bada put OI → rich players ne 430 put sell ki hai = "market 430 ke neeche nahi jaayegi; agar 430 aayi toh peti bhar ke stock kharid lenge." 500 pe bada call OI → "DAB 500 se upar nahi jaayega; sellers delivery dene ready hain."
  • Change in OI: Build-up (nayi positions ban rahi hain) vs Unwind (purane players position kaat rahe hain).

Volume vs OI (Biscuit shop example)

Mere paas 10 Parle-G biscuits hain.
Aankhein: 10 biscuit beche → 10 bheje → 10 wapas aaye → phir beche...
Volume = 30+ (kitni baar trade hua)
OI = 10 (kitne unique open contracts hain)
"Ek hi contract se 1000 crore ki volume kar sakta hoon — OI wahi rahega."

8.2 Max Pain

  • Max Pain strike = jis strike pe Call + Put combined OI sabse zyada hota hai.
  • Concept: market aksar expiry ke paas usi level pe close hota hai jahan most options expire worthless (sellers win).
  • Use: "jis strike pe combined premium/OI sabse zyada hai, market usko nahi todega." 99% chance market us range mein close hogi.
  • Teacher ka view: "Logically/factually correct hai, par main personally use nahi karta — mere ko isme itna alpha nahi dikhta. Main bahut normal tarike se kaam karta hoon."

8.3 Put-Call Ratio (PCR)

PCR = (Total Put OI) ÷ (Total Call OI)
PCR > 1 → market bullish (zyada put sellers = market girne nahi denge)
PCR < 1 → market bearish (zyada call sellers = market badhne nahi denge)
  • Q: "Zyada put seller = market neeche giregi?" FALSE. Put seller market ko flat/upar chahata hai. Call seller flat/neeche chahata hai.
  • Put seller 2 lakh, call seller 500 → PCR 4 → market down nahi jaayegi. Ulti condition → market upar nahi jaayegi.
  • Ye "big players ka game" hai — woh crores se trade karte hain, unki info/alpha pe bharosa karte hain. (Yahan 99% crowd rule ulta hai kyunki ye sellers hain, powerful players hain.)
Caution: "These are crowd behavior signals, not certainty. Use them with price action. Don't worship the option chain — read it but don't worship it."

9. Margin for Option Sellers

  • Buyer: Full cash premium pay (margin pe option buy nahi kar sakte). ₹10,000 ka option → ₹10,000 dena hoga.
  • Seller: Premium nahi deta — margin (SPAN + Exposure) girvi rakhta hai, phir aaram se option bechta hai.
  • Volatility badhe → margin requirement exchange apne aap badha deti hai (intraday bhi badal sakta hai).
  • Spread reduces margin: Put sell + neeche wali put buy → margin reduce (aage strategies mein detail).
  • Margin ke types (SPAN, Exposure, Delivery margin) — teacher: "Main nahi jaanta, jaanna nahi chahta. Merko sirf combined margin 1.32 crore blocked hai — wahi matter karta hai. Margin margin hai — paisa girvi rakho, baat khatam."
Reality Check: "Selling earns a small steady premium against a large posted margin. Risk management (not premium size) is what keeps you in the game." Long-run mein paisa wahin banega jo risk manage karega.

10-Second Recap

Options price = IV + TV. Put IV = max(Strike−Spot,0), Call IV = max(Spot−Strike,0). Moneyness: strike upar/neeche/equal. ATM ka theta & delta-max (0.5). Greeks: Delta (stock), Theta (time), Gamma (delta change), Vega (IV), Rho (interest). VIX = fear index — premium pehle badhta hai, phir VIX. Option chain = sab strikes ki table (OI, LTP, Greeks, IV). OI batata hai bade players kahin kahin "bood" baithe hain → market un levels ko nahi todta. Sell jab IV high ho, buy jab IV low.

12. Golden Rules / Take-aways

  1. Hamesha liquid contracts mein kaam karo. ITM contracts usually illiquid — unse door.
  2. Option seller = waiting ke paise. Theta din-raat seller ki jeb mein paisa daalta hai.
  3. Short put = large par limited risk; short call = unlimited risk. Kabhi naked call mat becho without plan.
  4. 99% retailers option buyers hain (hero-zero) — isliye 99% lose karte hain.
  5. Sell when IV is high, buy when IV is low. VIX 10–20% upar → put sell ka mauka.
  6. Sirf great-of-great companies (DAB, HUL, Reliance, HDFC Bank, top IT) mein put selling.
  7. Premiums pehle badalte hain, phir VIX. Books galat likhti hain — exam mein standard answer dena.
  8. Option chain ko padho, pooja mat karo — crowd signal hai, certainty nahi.
  9. OI/Max Pain/PCR = probability signals, use with price action.
  10. Risk management > premium size. "Jo risk manage karega, wahi long-run mein jeetega."
  11. Stop loss teacher ki dictionary mein nahi hai — risk management alag tarike se (next class: strategies, spreads, wheel, covered calls, taxes, monthly routine).
