1️⃣ Core Philosophy / Main Baatein

  • Options trading ka asli purpose: Option buyer apna risk option seller ke sar pe daalta hai — "Transfer of risk from option buyer to option seller." Seller ko is risk ke badle PAID kiya jaata hai (premium).
  • Risk = customization: Options se aap decide kar sakte ho ki "mujhe max ₹400 ka loss dekhna hai, chahe duniya tabaah ho jaye." Stock mein ye possible nahi — market 30% gire to 30% loss.
  • Trade-off rule: Zyada risk → zyada reward; low risk → kam paisa. Strategy choose karte waqt ye samjh ke karo.
  • Sellers ka edge: Sideways market mein seller profit karta hai (theta), buyer nahi.
  • Define karo, size karo, survive karo: Professional default = defined-risk spreads (Bull Put / Bear Call). Positions size worst-case loss se karo, sirf premium se nahi.
  • Survive first, compound second, eat theta — but NEVER bet the account. (Teacher ka signature line.)
  • Powerful players (HFTs) se ladna nahi: ya to unse door raho, ya unki team mein aa jao. Unse lad ke nahi jeet sakte.

Undefined Risk (naked selling)

  • Put becho, premium collect karo — worst case huge (stock ₹0 bhi ho sakta hai).
  • Example: DAB 450 put bechi, stock ₹0 chala gaya → ~₹4–5 lakh loss [hypothetical].
  • Teacher ka personal example: "Single gap down + news can wipe out months of profits" — Trump–Iran war ne unka 1.5–2 saal ka options profit shunya kar diya tha.

Notional Value (concept)

Notional = Stock/Future price × Minimum lot size (quantity) DAB ~₹400 [approx], lot size 1250 [approx] → Notional ≈ 400 × 1250 = ₹5,00,000 (5 lakh) Agar stock ₹600 ho gaya → 600 × 1250 = ₹7,50,000 (7–7.5 lakh)
  • India mein stock notional 5–6 lakh ke beech, index ka 12–13 lakh [approx] rakha jaata hai.
  • Lot size daily change nahi hota, price change hota hai → notional fluctuate karta hai.

Defined Risk (spread)

  • Put becho + door ki OTM put buy karo as insurance (hedge).
  • Premium thoda kam, par worst case hamesha capped, margin kam, chain ki neend.
📌 "The professional default for retail-sized clients" = spread. — Hemant Jain

6️⃣ Delta Neutral / Short Gamma Strategies [35:06–44:18]

  • Sirf put bechi → theta collect = ~₹13,000 max profit. Agar 1000% sure ho market yahin rahegi → same strike ki call bhi bech do.
  • Result: Margin same (~₹81,000) par max profit DOUBLE; theta double; delta ≈ 0.
  • Ye hai Delta Neutral Position = Short Gamma / Negative Gamma (sirf gamma bech rahe ho).
  • Risk: Market kisi bhi direction mein zyada gayi → tagda loss. Par healthy break-even range milti hai: ATM pe ~±5%; OTM put + OTM call becho to ~±14% volatility tolerance [approx].
  • Kab use karein: Jab market range-bound / sideways ho. "Profit from a quiet range-bound market by selling time and volatility."

7️⃣ Cash-Secured Put + Wheel Strategy (Ek-Slide Strategy) [44:19–52:26]

📌 Core rule: "Sell a cash-secured put on a stock you are happy to own if strike is below price." DAB 400 pe lena hai → 400 ki put becho; ITC 200 pe lena hai → 200 ki put; Nifty 22,000 tak jaayega → 22,000 ki put. Cash-secured = jeb mein itna cash ho ki assignment pe delivery le sako.

Month-end ke 2 outcomes:

  1. Not assigned (price strike ke neeche nahi aayi) → premium keep karo, agli cycle mein dobara put becho (repeat).
  2. Assigned → "Now you own a quality stock at a discount price." Delivery lo, phir calls becho (target strike pe).

DAB example:

DAB ₹450 hai, aap 420 pe lena chahte ho → 420 ki put becho. Month end: DAB 420 se neeche → delivery lo; nahi aaya → premium khao + agli month ki put becho.

Call kaunsi strike pe becho? (2 methods)

MethodKaiseDAB example
1. Envelope indicator (beginners)Length 200, Percentage 14; upper/lower lines bold karoLower blue ~₹420 (buy level); upper orange ~₹490–500 (call sell level) → 490/500 ki call
2. Resistance spotting (advanced)Jahan se stock baar-baar gira ho wahan call bechoDAB baar-baar 525 se gira → 520/525 ki call; lamba target → 580

💡 Q: Envelope target (500) aur option-chain target (500) same kyun? Vikas Sharma ka answer (correct): "Big players are also using the same approach." — Teacher bade players ki table pe baithta hai, wo bhi yehi karte hain.

Wheel Strategy (income at every stage)

  1. Put bechi → stock neeche aaya → stock mila (discount pe); neeche nahi aaya → premium mila.
  2. Call bechi (covered) → stock upar gaya → profit book; sideways/neeche → premium collect.
  3. Called away → profit + dobara wheel restart.
🎯 Teeno stages mein premium/profit — isliye "Wheel" best strategy. Called away = price sold-call se upar gaya → stock predefined price pe bech do (profit par), phir wheel restart.

8️⃣ Rollover — Colpal Example [52:27–58:50]

Definition: Mahina beet gaya, position minor loss mein hai → current-month position square off karo (sell tha to buy, buy tha to sell) aur agla month SAME STRIKE ki same position create karo.

Colpal example (step-by-step): 1. Colpal 2000 PUT ₹30 pe bechi [approx] 2. Month end: Colpal 2000 se thoda neeche; put ab ₹40 ki → ₹10 ka LOSS (40 − 30) 3. ROLLOVER: 2000 put ₹40 pe buy back (₹10 loss book) → Feb ki SAME 2000 put: intrinsic ₹40 + time value ₹2–10 [approx] → ₹50 pe becho 4. Agar stock 2000 se upar gaya → Feb put worthless → ₹0.05 pe buy back → ₹50 profit 5. NET = −10 + 50 = +₹40 PROFIT

Same strike kyun, neeche ki kyun nahi? (Bandita ka sawaal)

  • Neeche ki put (₹5–6 ki) bechi to market full recover hone pe bada profit nahi banega. Idea: market recover hone pe BADA profit banana hai. "It's always a trade-off."
  • Teacher: "Maine 30 ki bechi, 40 ki buy ki; ab 40 se mehengi bechunga — us level ka ziddi aadmi hoon; bada paisa kamaana hai."
  • Risk mein major difference nahi aata (5–10% neeche ki put pe bhi stock girne pe loss hota hai).
📌 Kuch positions 6–7–8 mahine roll hoti rehti hain. Puts usually month end tak hold karo (poora premium khaane ke liye). Agar beech mein 50–60% decay mil jaye → book karke next position mein jaao: "2 din mein 50% profit — good enough, take that, kisi aur stock ki put becho."

1️⃣3️⃣ Hedging — ₹3 Crore Portfolio Example [1:12:10–1:23:47]

Definition (teacher ka): "Hedging is simply BUYING PUT OPTIONS" = buying insurance.

  • Position hedging: Bull put spread (put becho + neeche wali OTM put kharido).
  • Portfolio hedging: poore portfolio ko insure karo.

₹3 Crore Portfolio hedge example (Nifty):

1. Nifty 50 ki ATM put 23,000 (December expiry) kharido 2. 23,000 put last price = ₹500; lot size = 65 3. Exposure protected per lot = 23,000 × 65 = ₹14,95,000 ≈ ₹15 LAKH 4. 3 crore hedge karne ke liye: 3,00,00,000 ÷ 15,00,000 = 20 LOTS 5. Cost = 20 × ₹500 × 65 = ₹6,50,000 (6.5 lakh) 6. % mein: 6.5 lakh ÷ 3 crore = 2.2% for ~7 months 7. Payoff: Nifty 5–10% giri → ₹30 lakh potential loss → sirf 2.2% cost pe khatam

Sasti alternative (22,000 put):

Support level logic: 2022 mein Nifty 22,000 ke aas-paas tha; support tootega to "lanka lag jayegi" — nahi tootega. 22,000 Dec put = ₹318 Cost = 20 × 318 × 65 = ₹4,13,400 ≈ ₹4.03 lakh = 1.3% of 3 crore 📌 "1.3% to pay kar sakte hain; hum saal mein 10–15–20% extra nikal lenge. Ye sirf black swan event se bachne ke liye hai."

Black Swan analogy (Chandni Chowk sari shop):

3 crore ki sariyon ka stock; ek din aag lag gayi → sab khatam. Har dukaan wala (sari/jewellery) insurance kharidta hai — "agar aag lag gayi to bank wale paisa de denge — peace of mind." Bina insurance ke aag lagi to 3–4 crore ka loss — 5 lakh ka premium kuch nahi. Yehi hedging hai.
📌 Hedging discipline: Put bech di / hedge kharid li → beech mein square off NAHI karna. Ya to December end pe kaato, ya market giri ho aur put achi profit mein ho tab kaato. "Aaj green candle ban gayi, hedging square off kar raha hoon" → aisa karne walon ka bahut bura kat-ta hai.

Expected returns (conservative): 25 lakh pe 4 positions, conservative play → mahine ka 1–1.5% → kisi position mein atko bhi to net 25–30% yearly aaram se.

1️⃣7️⃣ Golden Rules (DOS / DON'TS)

✅ DOS

  1. Sell when IV is high (fat premium, likely revert down).
  2. Hamesha credit spreads (Bull Put / Bear Call) — premium collect + risk defined.
  3. Quality stocks (Super 45, F&O available) mein hi kaam; gir hua stock + us din gir hua → best.
  4. Position sizing: 25L→4, 12L→2, 5–6L→1, 2–3L→1 (hedged).
  5. Limit orders hamesha; market orders kabhi nahi.
  6. Exit rule entry se pehle define karo; 50–60% decay/profit pe book karo (GTT laga sakte ho).
  7. Loss mein → rollover (expiry se 1 din pehle, same strike); profit mein → square off + nayi position.
  8. Puts ko month end tak hold karo (full premium khaane ke liye).
  9. Portfolio hedge karo (~2% cost) — black swan se bachao; hedge beech mein mat kholo-band karo.
  10. Entries/exits/reasons journal karo; F&O ko business income treat karke costs set off karo (CA ke saath).
  11. Option chain ka max-call-OI target + envelope/resistance — dono same aate hain (big players same approach).

❌ DON'TS

  1. Naked selling without position sizing — single gap down/news can wipe months of profits (Trump–Iran war example).
  2. Over-leverage — single biggest account killer (Rick Guerin).
  3. Illiquid strikes pe kaam nahi.
  4. Revenge trading kabhi nahi.
  5. Delta-neutral/short-gamma positions (strangle/straddle/butterfly) — brokerage kha jaati hai; tight-range butterfly = gambling (2–3% range).
  6. Big events ko ignore nahi; "market 50% nahi giregi" ka attitude nahi — tail event "will find you."
  7. Blindly average down nahi.
  8. Random chunn-munné stocks mein puts nahi.
  9. Market orders nahi.
  10. Hedge ko baar-baar square off/create nahi.
